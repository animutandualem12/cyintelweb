Title:  
Emerging AI Threats in Modern Cybersecurity

Category:  
News

Subcategory:  
AI, Cyber Defense, Threat Intelligence

Summary:  
Artificial intelligence is transforming cybersecurity, but it also introduces new risks. Attackers are leveraging AI to automate phishing, evade detection, and exploit vulnerabilities faster than ever before.

Full Content:  
Artificial intelligence (AI) has become a double-edged sword in cybersecurity. While defenders use AI to detect anomalies and predict attacks, adversaries are employing it to craft sophisticated, adaptive threats.
Recent studies highlight how generative models can produce realistic phishing emails and deepfake content that bypass traditional filters.
To counter these challenges, organizations are adopting AI-driven intrusion detection systems and explainable models that enhance transparency and trust.
As AI continues to evolve, collaboration between researchers, security professionals, and policymakers will be crucial to ensure a resilient digital ecosystem.
